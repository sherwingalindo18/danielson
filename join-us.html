Investing

"Dirt doesn't dip."

Land is disaster-proof. No tenants, no termites — and in our experience, it always appreciates. Set up a call if any of these sound like you:

  • You want to own land in Tennessee
  • You'd use your IRA to invest more profitably
  • You want to diversify with a quality hard asset
  • You're done with the tenant & toilet hassles
  • You're seeking a passive income stream
Partnering

Share a deal. Join a deal. Fund a deal.

After reviewing our portfolio, reach out if you'd like to partner on a Tennessee deal. We're always happy to review TN land. Our typical buy box:

  • Minimum of 20 acres
  • Ample road frontage
  • Gentle or mixed topography, no flood plains
  • Healthy real estate market
  • Tennessee only — preference for Middle TN & Nashville
