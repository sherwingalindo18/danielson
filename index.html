About Danielson Land Co.

We grew up doing land — and never stopped.

Raised by a father called Red Dog, the sisters spent weekends clearing fallen timber, walking the perimeter, and learning to read a deed at the campfire. When he passed, the land kept providing — and his lessons became a livelihood.

They started Danielson Land Co. with $20,000 from the sale of the family land. Today it is their way to build long-term wealth, model entrepreneurship for their kids, and spend more time in the nature they love. We're a two-person operation because our integrity and work ethic can't be outsourced.

What Drives Us

Mission, vision, and the way we value dirt.

Our Mission

Transform overlooked rural parcels into thoughtfully improved, build-ready land that families are proud to call home — while building lasting wealth for our own.

Our Vision

To be Middle Tennessee's most trusted land developers — known for straight-shooting integrity, quality partners, and deals people come back for.

Our Philosophy

"Dirt doesn't dip." Land is disaster-proof — no tenants, no termites, and in our experience it always appreciates. We invest in it, and we improve it, every single day.

The Development Process

From map-scrub to sold in four moves.

01

Acquisition

A sniper approach with map-scrubbing for quality land — direct from sellers, brokers, and our investor network.

02

Due Diligence

Zoning, road access, topography, wetlands, and soils — then a preliminary plat for the ideal lot split.

03

Value Adds

Survey, brush clearing, perc tests, culverts and drives — the improvements that unlock a parcel's highest use.

04

Disposition

Top brokers, drone footage, and nationwide marketing — targeting cash buyers at full retail within 90 days.
